After completing

1. **Assignment 1 (Paper Understanding)**

Each group must submit a **Final Project Report written in research paper format** and give a

**Plagiarism & AI-Generated Content Policy
1.** All submitted work must be **original**. The **maximum allowed similarity (plagiarism)** is
**10%** (excluding references and properly quoted material). Reports exceeding this limit
may be rejected or penalized.
**2.** Use of **AI-generated text (ChatGPT, Grammarly AI, etc.)** is allowed **only for
language improvement** , but the **technical writing, analysis, results, and explanations
must be written by students**. Any AI-assisted content must not exceed the **10% limit**
and students are responsible for ensuring correctness, originality, and proper citation.
**1. Objective of the Final Project Report**
The purpose of the final report is to combine all previous work into a **single structured research
paper** , similar to papers published in major NLP/DL or CV/DL conferences such as:
- ACL Conference on Computational Linguistics
- Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing
- CVPR
- IEEE
The report should demonstrate:
- understanding of the selected research paper


- ability to reproduce experiments
- ability to design and conduct new experiments
- clear analysis of experimental results
**2. Required Sections in the Final Report**
The report must be **10 – 12 pages** and include the following sections.
**1. Introduction**
Explain:
- the research problem
- motivation for the study
- overview of the selected research paper
**2. Background / Related Work**
Briefly explain:
- the original research paper
- related methods or approaches mentioned in the literature
**3. Proposed Model (Proposal Model)**
This is one of the **main required sections**.
Explain:
- the model you implemented or modified
- architectural details
- motivation behind your proposed changes or extension
Include **model diagrams if possible**.
**4. Experimental Setup**
Explain:
- datasets used
- preprocessing steps
- training setup
- hyperparameters


- tools or frameworks used such as PyTorch or Hugging Face.
**5. Results (Important Section)**
This section must include **two types of results** :
**1. Reproduced Results**
Results obtained when reproducing the experiments from the original research paper.
Example table:
**Model Paper Result Your Result
X X X**

**2.Additional Experiment Results**
Results obtained from the **new experiments proposed in Assignment 3**.
Students must compare:

- original model
- reproduced baseline
- proposed modification
Include **tables, graphs, and performance comparisons**.
**6. Discussion**
Explain:
- why your proposed method improved or did not improve performance
- limitations of the approach
- insights gained from experiments
**7. Conclusion**
Summarize:
- key findings of the project
- overall contributions
- possible future improvements
**8. References**


All sources must be properly cited, including:

- the original research paper
- datasets
- libraries or frameworks used
**3. Code and Experiment Submission**
Along with the report, groups must submit:
- project **code repository** (e.g., GitHub)
- experiment logs
- dataset preprocessing scripts
This ensures transparency and reproducibility of results.
**4. Project Presentation**
Each group must give a **project presentation**.
**Presentation Duration
10 – 15 minutes per group**

**Presentation Content**
The presentation should include:

1. Problem and motivation
2. Overview of the selected research paper
3. Reproduced experiments
4. Proposed method or modification
5. Additional experiments conducted
6. Results and analysis
7. Key conclusions
Students should include **figures, diagrams, and result tables** in their slides.
**5. Individual Understanding Requirement**
Although the project is done in groups, **each student must understand the full project**.
During or after the presentation, the instructor may ask **questions to individual members** about:


- the methodology
- experimental setup
- results and analysis
Students should be able to **explain their work clearly**.
